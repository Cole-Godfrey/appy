# Example prompts

Run from this checkout after completing setup. Edit scope and preferences before use.

## Shortlist

```text
Follow APPLICATION_GUIDE.md and read INFORMATION.md and QUESTIONS.md.
Export my complete configured Notion tracker to tmp/tracker.json.
Run public discovery with --theirstack-limit 0. Check live eligibility
and return five matching roles with URLs and any unanswered questions.
Do not fill or submit forms. Clean up temporary exports when finished.
```

## One reviewed application

```text
Prepare one application for this role: [URL]. Follow APPLICATION_GUIDE.md.
Check Notion for duplicates and use my documented facts and resume.
Use Chrome and Simplify, and Gmail MCP for a requested verification code.
Stop before submission so I can review the form. Ask about missing answers.
```

## Authorized batch

```text
Apply to up to three eligible roles matching INFORMATION.md.
Follow APPLICATION_GUIDE.md, export the full Notion tracker, and use
public discovery only. You may submit after reviewing every field
against my documented facts. Stop on missing answers or new consent
decisions. Record each confirmed receipt in Notion and verify the row.
Close completed tabs and clean up the batch's temporary files.
```

## Email catch-up

```text
Review recruiting emails delivered after [date and time zone]. Verify
the connected Gmail account, paginate the search, and follow
EMAIL_MONITOR.md for matching and stage rules. Update the configured
Notion tracker and NEXT-STEPS.md. Preserve later evidence and checked
tasks. Do not send, delete, archive, or relabel mail.
```
