# Community conduct

Treat contributors and job seekers with respect. Critique the work, explain disagreements, and respect people's time and privacy. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, and unwanted contact are unacceptable.

Do not share another person's application records, email, or credentials. Do not use community spaces for spam, purchased stars, or coordinated voting.

Use GitHub's Report content control for abusive content. Maintainers may remove content, close discussions, or restrict participation when needed. This applies in project issues, pull requests, and other spaces managed by the project.
